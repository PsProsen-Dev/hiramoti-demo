Our Story

Rooted in Bamboo, Raised by Nature

A quiet corner of Mouamari, Maynaguri — where hand-carved bamboo, still pond water and homely Bengali cooking come together into one unforgettable family retreat.

★ 4.2★
267 Google reviews

Hira Moti Restaurant & Bamboo Hut began as a family dream in Uttar Mauamari — a bamboo retreat where the dining hall itself is a work of art, its walls lined with hand-carved bamboo panels crafted by local hands.

Step outside and the retreat unfolds: bamboo stilt huts rise over the pond, paddle boats drift on still water, and garden swings sway under open skies. It has become a favourite picnic and family-getaway spot in Maynaguri — the kind of place where lunch stretches into a lazy afternoon.
