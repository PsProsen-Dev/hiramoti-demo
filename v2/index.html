Welcome

Welcome to Hira Moti

Our Story

Built of bamboo, run with heart

Tucked into Uttar Mauamari's greenery, Hira Moti began as a simple riverside eatery and grew — hut by hut — into one of the area's most loved family dining spots.

Every structure here is hand-built from bamboo: the dining halls, the stilt huts over the water, the night-lit decks. It's rustic by design, and warm by nature.
