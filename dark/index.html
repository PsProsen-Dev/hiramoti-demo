Our Story

A Hideaway Built
from Bamboo & Water

Nestled on the banks at Uttar Mauamari, Maynaguri, Hira Moti Restaurant & Bamboo Hut is where Bengal's love for food meets the quiet of the water. Hand-woven bamboo walls, stilt huts over the pond, and string lights that turn every evening golden.

Families come for the Bengali curries and biryani, linger over chowmein and tandoor breads, then end the night with boating and tea by the deck. Nobody leaves in a hurry.
