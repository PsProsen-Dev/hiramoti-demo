Our Story

A Bamboo Paradise in Maynaguri

Tucked among bamboo groves by the water in Uttar Mauamari, Hira Moti is a family restaurant & homestay unlike anything else in Maynaguri. Guests dine in bamboo-stilt huts raised over the pond, drift through the day on pedal boats, and linger at swings, picnic decks and shaded gazebos made for slow afternoons.

Known far and wide for authentic Bengali family cuisine, it has become a favourite outing for families and couples alike — a whole day's escape, wrapped in green and gold.
