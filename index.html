Our Story

Dinner in a bamboo stilt hut, over the water

Tucked away in Uttar Mauamari near Maynaguri, Hira Moti is built almost entirely of bamboo — carved ceilings, woven walls, stilt huts that stand right over the water. By day it is a breezy family picnic spot; by night the lantern-lit deck turns magical.

Families come for honest Bengali cooking at honest prices, kids come for the pedal boats, the swings and the fishing pond — and everyone leaves with photos. Much of what lands on your plate is grown on our own organic farm patch.
